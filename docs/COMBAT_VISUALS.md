# Combat visuals

COMBAT VISUALS appears in launcher Options > Game screen and HUD and
Settings > SPIDY VR > DISPLAY. It defaults on and saves as combat_visuals
in spidy_vr.ini and shared profiles.

Off hides Spidy's punch-direction arrows, white target/lock circle,
impact bursts and combo lettering, the BLOCKED mark of a blow a guard
stopped (a shield in the comic style, a shield and a tag of diamonds in
the tech style), KO text and automatic combat slow-motion
recolouring/ripples. Manual slow motion keeps its own visuals.

A blow a guard stops ends the combo whatever this switch says: the
flow's count starts over, and with it the arrow of the blow it called
next (left, right, up or down). The marks only show it.

Spider-Sense is independent of this switch: overhead attack warnings,
offscreen direction indicators and sightline warnings remain enabled for
counter, dodge and jump attacks in both Comic and Tech styles. The switch
does not filter attacks by weapon type.

Combat controls, target selection, directional bonuses, counters, damage,
sounds and haptics keep working. Hand web-aim markers, interaction indicators
and the game's own HUD retain their existing controls.

Settings/profile checks cover defaults, persistence, import/export and
independence from combat and web aiming. The rendering paths were checked
against the existing Spider-Sense behavior; final in-headset verification
of this option remains necessary. The player's setup is Quest 3 through
Virtual Desktop.
